# Cyber Legal Guidance System

An intelligent application that maps cyber crimes to BNS & IT Act sections. It provides immediate emergency response steps and acts as a legal first-aid system for victims.

## ⚖️ Key Modules

### 1. Automated Crime-to-Law Mapping
* **The Challenge:** Victims of cybercrimes often don't know which specific legal sections apply to their situation.
* **The Action:** Developed a semantic mapping engine that takes natural language queries and cross-references them against a structured database of the BNS and IT Act.
* **The Result:** Instantly provides relevant legal sections, saving time for both victims and legal professionals.

### 2. Legal First-Aid & Emergency Steps
* **The Challenge:** Immediate actions are critical after a cyber incident, but reliable guidance is scattered.
* **The Action:** Integrated actionable "Emergency Steps" tailored to the specific type of cybercrime (e.g., preserving digital evidence, contacting the 1930 helpline).
* **The Result:** Empowers victims with a clear, step-by-step recovery plan.
